Vernacular First-Generation Learner Support Platform
🧾 Problem Statement

Approximately 45% of undergraduate students in Indian government universities are first-generation college students — the first in their family to attend higher education.

These students enter institutions where instruction, textbooks, and academic culture operate in English, while their foundational education was in regional language medium schools.

This creates a language–knowledge gap, leading to academic failure due to language barriers rather than lack of intelligence.

First-generation students also lack implicit academic knowledge typically gained through family background:

Understanding exam question types
Structuring academic answers
Effective study strategies for retention
Navigating institutional processes
Accessing eligible scholarships

Existing translation tools fail because:

Academic language ≠ conversational language
Technical terms carry precise meanings
Direct translation loses context

👉 What is needed is bilingual academic bridging, not translation.

🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered platform that:

Bridges vernacular ↔ academic English gap
Explains concepts clearly in both languages
Supports exam preparation intelligently
Improves academic writing skills
Provides awareness of entitlements and processes
