Project Title
Vernacular Bridge: Bilingual Academic Bridging & Exam Preparation Intelligence
Tagline
Not just translation. A Cognitive Bridge that turns Tamil-medium understanding into academic English mastery for first-generation students.
1. 🎯 Problem Statement
A Tamil-medium student can clearly explain a concept in their mother tongue, but fails to express the same answer in academic English during exams.
This gap is not due to lack of understanding, but lack of language bridging.
Approximately 45% of undergraduate students in Indian government universities are first-generation college students — the first in their family to attend higher education. They arrive with strong foundational knowledge from Tamil-medium schools, but higher education operates entirely in English — textbooks, instruction, and examinations.
The resulting language-knowledge gap leads to academic failure attributable to language barriers rather than intellectual incapacity.
What first-generation learners need is not translation, but bilingual academic bridging — concept explanation in both languages with culturally situated examples and explicit academic language development support.
2. 🎯 Objective
To build a Cognitive Bridge System that transforms a student’s Tamil-medium understanding into confident academic English performance through structured bilingual explanations, exam question decoding, answer templating, and intelligent exam preparation.
3. 👤 Target Users
First-generation undergraduate students from Tamil-medium school backgrounds entering English-medium higher education in Tamil Nadu and South India.

5. ⚙️ Core Features (Focused Hackathon Scope)
Must-Have Features

Bilingual Concept Explainer: Delivers simple, neutral, textbook-style Tamil (with culturally situated examples and analogies) + precise academic English with clear steps.
Academic Question Decoder: Identifies question type (define, explain, compare, analyse, etc.) and provides a ready section-by-section response template.
Exam Preparation Intelligence: Generates medium-difficulty, concept-based MCQs directly from the explanation. These include:
Concept understanding checks
Common misconception traps
Application-based questions

Writing Coach Lite (stretch goal): Basic structured feedback on short student draft answers.

7. 🛠️ Zero-Cost, Fully Local Tech Stack

Inference: llama.cpp server (preferred) or Ollama
Model: Qwen3-8B-Instruct or Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q4_K_M GGUF
Structured Output: GBNF grammar + max_tokens=1200
Frontend: Gradio (fastest for demo) or Streamlit
Backend: FastAPI Orchestrator
Cache: Pre-cached demo queries (visible + hidden backups)

Key Advantage: Works completely offline — making it accessible to rural and low-connectivity students.
8. 🔄 Robustness

Handles mixed Tamil-English (Tanglish) inputs through intelligent preprocessing
Requests clarification for highly ambiguous questions
Falls back gracefully to simpler explanations when complex generation fails
Cold-start warm-up, input disabling during generation, and single concurrent request limit for stability

9. 📊 Risks & Mitigation

Hallucination → Controlled via structured outputs, GBNF grammar, and multi-layer validation checks
Language inconsistency → Enforced through strict prompt design, simple Tamil constraints, and quality checks
Factual drift → NCERT-level accuracy bias and internal consistency validation
