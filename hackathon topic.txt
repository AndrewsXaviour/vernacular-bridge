PS-21: Vernacular First-Generation Learner Support Platform
📌 Title

Vernacular First-Generation Learner Support Platform with Academic Language Bridging, Concept Translation, and Exam Preparation Intelligence

🧾 Problem Statement (Full Meaning)

A large number of students in India are:

First-generation learners (no academic support at home)
Studying in regional language backgrounds
But required to learn in English-medium education systems

This creates a major gap:

They understand concepts in their native language
But struggle with academic English terminology
Leads to:
Poor comprehension
Low confidence
Drop in performance
🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered learning platform that:

Bridges vernacular ↔ academic English gap
Helps students understand concepts clearly
Supports exam preparation intelligently
Makes learning accessible and personalized
🧠 Core Features Expected
1. 🌐 Academic Language Bridging
Convert:
English textbook content → regional language
Regional doubts → academic English
Focus on meaning, not just translation
2. 📘 Concept Simplification
Explain concepts:
In simple terms
In native language
Step-by-step
3. 🔁 Concept Translation Engine
Translate:
Complex academic sentences → easy language
Maintain:
Context
Subject accuracy
4. 📝 Exam Preparation Intelligence
Generate:
Practice questions
MCQs / short answers
Important topics
Provide:
Personalized study guidance
5. 🧑‍🎓 First-Gen Learner Support
Features like:
Doubt clarification chatbot
Voice-based explanations
Learning path guidance
⚙️ Expected Technologies
NLP (translation + simplification)
LLMs (concept explanation, Q&A)
Speech (optional: voice input/output)
Personalization systems
⚠️ Key Constraints
Must support multiple Indian languages
Should be simple and accessible
Focus on clarity over complexity
Must work for low-resource students
🧠 Example Use Case

Input:

“Explain photosynthesis”

Output:

Tamil explanation (simple language)
English explanation (exam-ready)
Diagram (optional)
5 practice questions
“Explain like I’m 10 years old” version
